Welcome to Casa Del Marga

Casa Del Marga is a private resort located in Happy Valley, Brgy. Ibabang Dupay, Lucena City, Quezon.

Casa Del Marga provides a relaxing place where families and friends can spend quality time together.

The resort is suitable for birthdays, reunions, family gatherings and other special occasions.

Our goal is to give our guests a comfortable place where they can relax and create wonderful memories.
